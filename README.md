**H3: Why do AI course fees vary so much?**

Explain the eight cost drivers in 2–3 sentences each, with the fee impact of each stated directionally:

1. **Delivery format** — live instructor hours are the single biggest cost; recorded content is near-zero marginal cost.

2. **Mentorship and code review** — human feedback scales badly; programs with 1:1 mentors and code review price accordingly.

3. **Credential source** — university and IIT tie-ups carry licensing and revenue-share costs that are passed to the learner.

4. **Placement operations** — dedicated placement teams, partner networks and interview prep are expensive to run; premium bootcamps price for them.

5. **Program length** — 18-month programs cost more than 6-month ones, but not necessarily per capability gained.

6. **Curriculum currency** — continuously updated GenAI/agents/MLOps content requires ongoing instructor investment; static 2023 content doesn't.

7. **Physical infrastructure** — offline centres carry rent, hardware and local staff costs.

8. **Marketing and sales spend** — a meaningful share of premium fees funds acquisition, not instruction. State this factually without naming providers.
